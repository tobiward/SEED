# Seed

Smart Eating Every Day. A free diet tracker that installs on your phone's home screen from the browser, with no app store.

## Features

- Log foods with an amount and a measure (grams, ounces, fluid ounces, cups, servings)
- Calorie and protein rings that fill through the day
- Recipes: save a food once with per-unit calories and protein, then type only the amount
- Weight tracking and automatic protein, calorie and tracking streaks
- Look up foods by name or barcode in Open Food Facts: scan with the camera, take a photo of the barcode, or type the number
- Log a food again with one tap
- Optional extra nutrients (carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium), each switched on in the User tab with its own daily total and graph
- Search your saved foods, sorted A to Z
- Weight, calorie and protein graphs by week, month and year
- Works offline
- Backup and restore your data as a JSON file

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you log is stored in your browser's local storage, not on a server. The only thing that leaves your device is the search text or barcode number when you use the food lookup, which is sent to Open Food Facts. Camera images are processed on your device and never uploaded. Use **User > Export backup** now and then, especially on iPhone, since browsers can clear storage for sites you do not visit.

## Install

Open the site, then:

- **Android (Chrome):** menu > Install app
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share > Add to Home Screen
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** install icon in the address bar
